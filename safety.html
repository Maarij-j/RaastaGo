visibility Mode awareness

Practical reminders for safer travel—before, during, and after your ride.

directions_bus Metro (Bus) awareness

  • check_circle Keep bags in front and phone secure near doors.
  • check_circle Stand behind the line; let passengers exit first.
  • check_circle Hold a rail; avoid leaning on doors.
  • check_circle Move away from the curbside when boarding/alighting.
  • check_circle If you feel unsafe, change seats/coach area and call for help.

train Orange Line (Train) awareness

  • check_circle Keep distance from platform edge; avoid rushing the doors.
  • check_circle Mind the gap—step carefully when boarding.
  • check_circle Keep valuables zipped; stay alert in crowded coaches.
  • check_circle Note the station name before exiting; don’t follow strangers.
  • check_circle If separated from companions, agree on a meeting point (next station).

local_taxi Taxi awareness

  • check_circle Confirm vehicle and driver details before getting in.
  • check_circle Share trip details/location with a trusted contact.
  • check_circle Sit in the back seat when riding alone when possible.
  • check_circle Keep doors locked and windows mostly up.
  • check_circle Trust your instincts—end the ride in a safe public place if needed.

pedal_bike Bike awareness

  • check_circle Wear a helmet and visible clothing (even for short trips).
  • check_circle Avoid blind spots; assume drivers may not see you.
  • check_circle Use a well-lit route at night; keep phone away while riding.
  • check_circle Keep a safe buffer from parked cars (door opening risk).
  • check_circle Slow down at intersections and wet patches.

local_fire_department In Case of Fire

If you notice smoke or fire

  • check_circle Alert the driver immediately if safe to do so.
  • check_circle Move away from the source of smoke.
  • check_circle Cover your nose and mouth with a cloth.
  • check_circle Keep low to the floor where air is cleaner.

If exits are blocked

  • check_circle Locate the emergency hammer near windows.
  • check_circle Strike the corner of the window glass firmly.
  • check_circle Push the glass out carefully.
  • check_circle Exit quickly and help others if possible.

car_crash Road Accident

If there’s an accident

  • check_circle Check yourself and others for injuries.
  • check_circle Call Rescue 1122 immediately for medical help.
  • check_circle Do not move injured persons unless in immediate danger.
  • check_circle Move to a safe spot off the road if uninjured.

security Digital Security

Digital safety essentials

  • check_circle Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking apps or sensitive transactions.
  • check_circle RaastaGo will never ask for your password or OTP via call or SMS.
  • check_circle Keep location sharing on for trusted contacts during late travel.
